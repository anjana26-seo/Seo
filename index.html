SEO Audit + CRO
Bonnie's Bakery
Optimizing the site to bring more users to get high click-throughs and conversions
📸 Audit Evidence

↑ Left: Missing 'Buy Now' button — users stuck at 'Select Options'. Right: Performance score 60 with 8.7s Speed Index.

Target Audience
  • Moms, Couples, Gift Buyers, and Celebrity Shoppers
  • Local customers looking for quality, aesthetics, and affordable pricing
The Challenge
  • No "Buy Now" button — users forced into ambiguous "Select Option" flow with no clear path to purchase
  • Text overlapping product images with low contrast, making menu hard to read
  • Missing H1 heading structure, zero Alt text across 12 images
  • No Meta Description — invisible to local Google searches
  • Google Analytics & Facebook Pixel entirely absent
Strategic Solutions
1
Added prominent "Buy Now" & "Complete Your Order" buttons directly next to product options for frictionless checkout
2
Applied darker background overlays beneath text on product images for clear, professional readability
3
Pivoted copy to benefit-driven emotional hooks: "Your celebrations, made unforgettable" and "Memories you can taste"
4
Injected proper H1–H6 heading hierarchy + Meta Description: "Explore premium cakes and fresh bakery treats in Mumbai"
5
Added descriptive Alt text to all 12 images to capture Google Image search traffic
6
Installed Google Analytics + Facebook Pixel to track drop-off and run local retargeting ads
SEO Strategy
ASK The Event Associates
SEO Audit & Competitive Growth Strategy
📸 Audit Evidence

↑ The ASK Event Associates portfolio page — completely blank, losing visitor trust on arrival.

Target Audience
  • Everyday Families — seeking trusted local experts for weddings and personal milestones
  • Local Businesses — corporate clients using high-intent keywords like "best" or "expert"
The Challenge
  • Great load speed (0.85s) but search engines nearly blind — no meta description, canonical tags, or heading keywords
  • Competitor plagued by 21-second load time and 52 broken links — a massive untapped opportunity
  • Portfolio section completely blank — losing visitor trust instantly on arrival
Strategic Solutions
1
Fix Portfolio: Upload event photos to "Manage Portfolio" to instantly establish credibility and trust
2
Write a 1–3 sentence active-voice Meta Description with local high-intent keywords: "Best Event Organiser in Navi Mumbai"
3
Inject keywords into H1–H3 headings and image alt texts for every uploaded photo
4
Exploit competitor weakness (21s load + 52 broken links) by maintaining a clean, fast, optimized presence to dominate local rankings
